EtoMi: get your money back, the right way.

The multilingual AI consumer-rights assistant that helps Nigerians recover money from failed transfers, double debits, ATM and POS errors and wrong charges. “Ẹ̀tọ́ mi” means “my right” in Yorùbá.

One app, five names

MyRight · EtoMi · HakkiNa · IkikeM · NaMyRight

EtoMi speaks the customer's language: English, Yorùbá, Hausa, Igbo and Pidgin, with a name in each. It runs in any phone browser, installs like an app, and is free for individuals.

  • Explains your rights in plain words
  • Applies the right CBN rule and deadline
  • Writes, sends and tracks the complaint for you

How it works

Four steps from problem to refund

Tell it what happened

Type it, speak it, or snap the receipt, in your language.

It checks your rights

AI reads the story, keeps your bank and the recipient's bank apart, and applies the right CBN rule and deadline.

It sends the complaint

EtoMi emails your bank's complaints desk, with you in copy, and saves the case.

It follows up

Reminders every two days, a reminder letter when the bank's time runs out, then a ready CBN petition.

Features

What's inside EtoMi

Speak, type or snap

Voice input in five languages, typing, or a photo of the receipt or screenshot.

Knows the CBN rules

Rules and deadlines for failed transfers, ATM, POS and web payments, and wrong charges.

Keeps banks apart

Separates your bank from the recipient's bank, so the complaint goes to the right desk.

Ready letters

The complaint to your bank, a reminder letter, and a petition to the CBN.

Sends for you

Straight to your bank's complaints desk, with you in copy.

Follows up

Check-ins every two days and a deadline alert, as EtoMi's own notifications.

Works offline demo

Example cases you can explore with no connection.

Safe by design

Never asks for your PIN, password, OTP or BVN. Complaint text and images aren't stored.

My cases

Every complaint, tracked until it's resolved

EtoMi keeps your cases on your phone with the bank's deadline, what has been sent, and what happens next. When the money is back, you close the case.

  • Deadline countdown for every case
  • Reminder letter and CBN petition when needed
  • Open a case straight from a notification
