Privacy

Privacy notice

Last updated 24 November 2026

This website

drspecimen.online is an information website. It doesn't use cookies or adverts, and it doesn't store anything you type. It counts visits anonymously: your browser makes a random code for itself, and we see only how many visits there were and where they came from (for example, a QR code on our flyer), and whether the contact form was used. We never see your name, contact details or internet address. The contact form opens your own email app; nothing is sent to us until you press send there.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your name, email address and message only to reply to you and to handle your request. We don't sell or share them, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.

EtoMi

EtoMi has its own privacy rules, shown inside the app. In short: it never asks for your PIN, password, OTP, CVV or BVN; it doesn't store your complaint text or receipt images; and it sends a complaint only when you tap to send it.

Giving through Paystack

If you give or sponsor online, you pay on Paystack's secure page, not on this website. Paystack handles your card, bank or USSD details under its own privacy policy; DIG never sees them. We receive your name (if you give it), your email, the amount and the payment reference, and use them only to send receipts, thank you, and keep our accounts.

Fonts

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers to download them.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email research@drspecimen.online.
