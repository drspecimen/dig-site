ProductNov 2026

EtoMi now sends complaints and follows up by itself

The latest EtoMi release closes the loop from problem to refund:

  • Sent from EtoMi. Complaints go straight to your bank's complaints desk from EtoMi's own address, with you in copy so the bank replies to you.
  • Saved automatically. Every case is saved as soon as it's created, with the CBN deadline clock started.
  • Its own reminders. Every two days EtoMi asks whether your bank has fixed it. When the bank's time runs out it prepares a reminder letter, then a petition to the CBN's Consumer Protection Department.
ResearchNov 2026

Why bank complaints stall, and what we're doing about it

About ₦1.07 quadrillion moved electronically in Nigeria in 2024, across 11.2 billion transactions (NIBSS data reported by Nairametrics). When a transfer fails, a POS debits twice or an ATM doesn't pay, CBN rules protect the customer.

In 2025, 23,129 consumer complaints reached the Central Bank of Nigeria, and refunds of ₦19.12 billion and US$329.3 million were ordered (CBN 2025 Annual Report, reported by Legit.ng). But those numbers only count people who complained correctly and on time.

Our field conversations point to three barriers: people don't know the rules, don't know the deadlines, and don't know how to write the complaint. EtoMi was designed around exactly those three gaps, in the languages people actually speak.

ProductNov 2026

EtoMi installs like an app and works in five languages

EtoMi runs in any phone browser and can be installed to the home screen like an app. You can type, speak or snap your receipt, and EtoMi can read its answers aloud.

A demo mode with example cases works even without a connection, which makes it easy to show friends and family how it works. Fair-use limits per device keep the service free and available for everyone.

Sources: NIBSS e-payment data for 2024 (reported by Nairametrics, Jan 2025); CBN 2025 Annual Report (reported by Legit.ng, Jul 2026).
